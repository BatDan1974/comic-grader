How grades are worked out

The AI only finds defects. The app then grades them with fixed rules based on the standard grading tables: each defect type and level has a highest grade it allows, and the book gets the highest grade that every defect allows. As in the tables, one defect at its limit is fine "if others are absent"; several other defects at the same level cost up to two extra steps. Bindery flaws and dates/stamps stop counting below 4.5. Photos can't confirm 9.9 or 10.0, so 9.8 is the top. The rules were checked against 54 annotated examples from the grading guide: 49 match exactly and the other 5 are within one step.
